VOYAGE LOG  ·  PRE-MISSION BRIEFING
JASON & THE ARGONAUTS
A long time ago in ancient Greece, a king died — and his two sons began to fight over who would rule. The rightful heir was Aeson, the older son. But his half-brother Pelias wanted the throne for himself.
Pelias forced Aeson to give up the crown. To protect his newborn son from danger, Aeson secretly sent the baby to live with Chiron — a wise centaur in the mountains who trained heroes. That baby was Jason.
Pelias visited an oracle to ask about his future. The prophecy frightened him: "Beware the man who arrives wearing only one sandal." From that day forward, Pelias stared at every stranger's feet.
Twenty years later, Jason came of age. He set out to reclaim his father's throne. Along the way he helped an old woman cross a river — and lost a sandal in the current. The old woman was Hera, queen of the gods, testing whether Jason was worthy of her protection.
When Jason arrived at the palace with one sandal missing, Pelias recognized the prophecy. Desperate to be rid of him, the king made an offer: "Bring me the Golden Fleece from the land of Colchis, and the throne is yours." Jason agreed. What followed was the greatest voyage in Greek mythology.

— NAMES TO KNOW —
JASON
The hero. Rightful heir to the throne of Iolcus. Raised in secret by Chiron. Arrives with one sandal — exactly as the prophecy warned.
PELIAS
Jason's uncle. Stole the throne. Sends Jason on an impossible quest hoping he will die before he can take back what's his.
HERA
Queen of the gods. Disguises herself as an old woman to test Jason's character. Becomes his divine protector throughout the voyage.
CHIRON
The wise centaur who raised Jason in the mountains. Trained him as a warrior and hunter. Revealed his true identity when he came of age.
GOLDEN FLEECE
The magical golden ram's fleece, kept in the distant land of Colchis. Pelias says Jason can have the throne if he retrieves it. No one has ever survived the attempt.
